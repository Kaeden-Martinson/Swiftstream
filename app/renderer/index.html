    Connect your first server

    SwiftSSH gives you a terminal and fast file transfers to your servers. Uploads and downloads go over HTTPS on several connections at once, and unchanged files are skipped.

    1. Log in with SSH once. SwiftSSH starts itself on the server and remembers it.
    2. Open a terminal or browse files. Drag files in to upload them.
